How these pages are made and checked

python3 -I tools/build_trail.py writes these pages from the published files: the claim table, the receipts, the specs, the proof files and the modules. python3 -I tools/check_trail.py checks them without using the generator: every relative link in these pages resolves (links to websites, GitHub included, are not followed), every quoted statement is the spec text at the stated lines, every proof line names its theorem, every receipt entry says proved and matched, and every byte range and hex line is the module's bytes. The pages show what the files record; that the files are the ones the runs used is tools/check_tree.py's job, and re-running a proof is the replay's.
